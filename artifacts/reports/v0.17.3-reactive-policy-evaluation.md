## Interpretation

At peak prices, Pro-only costs 2.78 times Flash-only per verified task. Flash-then-Pro reduces cost per verified task by 55.3% relative to Pro-only while preserving its observed verified-success rate. It costs 24.2% more than Flash-only because failed Flash attempts are retained in task cost.

The independent-retry proxy is not evidence for a production repair policy. A real Flash repair receives failure evidence and must be measured as one joined trajectory before its economics can qualify.

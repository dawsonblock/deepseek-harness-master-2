## Design

- 15 task classes across 8 categories (factual, code, reasoning, debugging, structured, planning, verification, long-context).
- Three policies: Flash-only, Pro-only, current-router.
- Cold/warm separation: cold runs use fresh contexts, warm runs after warm-up.
- Structured verification criteria per task (same status vocabulary as RoutingOutcome).
- Per-run cache hit/miss/rate; cacheComparable flag when >10pp difference.
- Core metric: CostPerVerifiedTask = TotalCost / VerifiedPasses.
- ProNecessityRate, ProWasteRate, FlashRescueCost.
- Median and p90 latency/cost alongside arithmetic means.
